The end of the traceback says why: `Invalid assignment of ROF_OCN_MAPPING_RMAX to 5000.0. Reason: ROF_OCN_MAPPING_RMAX must be less than or equal to 4000 km.` When rmax and fold are still unset, `RunoffMappingGenerator` pre-fills mom6_forge's suggested values, and on this grid the suggestion (5000 km) was over the GUI's limit. The GUI should skip or clamp a suggestion outside the allowed range. The suggestion function itself is being looked at in NCAR/mom6_forge#163, where it comes out at or below 0 on some grids.
